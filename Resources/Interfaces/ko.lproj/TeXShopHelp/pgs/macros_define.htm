New items are created with the + button below the list, and new submenus and separators with the menu at its right end (⊙), which also duplicates the selected items; the − button deletes them. A little practice illustrates the basic behavior of the editor. Submenus can be created to any level.

To assign a keyboard shortcut to an item, click the Shortcut field and press the shortcut: Command or Control with a key, or one of F1–F20, with Shift and Option as needed. The ⊗ button in the field, or Delete while the field waits for the keys, removes the shortcut; Escape keeps the one it had. The result will immediately appear in the outline view. However, these assignments cannot duplicate key combinations already used by TeXShop menus. If they do, the new keyboard shortcut will be ignored.

In the contents, the placeholders that a macro replaces (#SEL#, #INS#, #PASTE#, the marks •, and #FILEPATH# and the other paths of an AppleScript macro) are shown as rounded tokens which say what they stand for, such as “Selected Text”. The macro keeps them as they are written. Type a placeholder, or choose it from the Insert menu below the contents; the help button at the right lists them all. Deleting next to a token deletes all of it.
