Toolbar and AppleScript

The selection and location of tools at the top of the TeX Source and PDF Preview windows can be modified using the menu item "Customize Toolbar." This toolbar support is entirely the work of Anton Leuski. Thanks!

The Preview window can show Zoom to Content and Zoom to Content Width in its toolbar; add them with "Customize Toolbar." They are also at the end of Preview > Magnification, where keyboard shortcuts can be assigned to them in Settings. Zoom to Content fits the typeset part of the page in view, without the white margins, in the window. Zoom to Content Width makes the typeset part as wide as the window and shows its top at the top of the window, so a page number or footnote at the foot of the page does not make the text smaller. In two-page layouts both pages of the row are measured; with one page across, the next page is measured too, so that a document printed on both sides keeps its line ends on every page. A rotated page is measured as it is shown. Like the Magnification tool, the buttons switch the window to a fixed magnification.

Fit to Content Width, in Preview > Magnification and in Settings, keeps the typeset part of all pages as wide as the window, like Fit to Window without the white margins: resizing the window changes the magnification to keep it so. These commands were suggested by Uwe Schmock.

TeXShop supports Applescript. This is also the work of Anton Leuski.
