Rearranging the Macro Menu

Open the Macro Editor (Macros › Open Macro Editor…). The list on the left shows the Macros menu as an outline, like the list view of a Finder window. To move an item, drag it to another place; drag it left or right to place it at another level, inside or outside a submenu.

To rename an item, select it and press Return, or click its name once more after a moment, as in the Finder. Type the new name and press Return; Escape keeps the old name.
