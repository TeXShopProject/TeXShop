\section{Adding Waves}\label{sec:waves}
A complicated signal can be seen as a sum of simple oscillations.
For a function of period $2\pi$, the Fourier series is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:series}
  f(x) \sim \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \bigl(a_n\cos nx + b_n\sin nx\bigr).
\end{equation}
