\section{Convergence}\label{sec:convergence}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:dirichlet}
  If $f$ is piecewise smooth, the series \eqref{eq:series} converges to
  $\frac12\bigl(f(x+0)+f(x-0)\bigr)$ at every point.
\end{theorem}
Near a jump, the partial sums of \eqref{eq:series} overshoot by about 9\%.
