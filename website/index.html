THE PROJECT

Continuing a
quarter-century of work.

Richard Koch created TeXShop in 2000. With contributions from many developers and users, it has become part of the daily work of people writing mathematics, science, and much more.

At Richard’s invitation, Yusuke Terada is taking over the development and maintenance of TeXShop. This site is being prepared as its new home.

“I have used TeXShop every day for a quarter of a century. I would love to help carry it forward into the next quarter century.”Yusuke Terada

Our thanks to Richard Koch and everyone who has helped develop, translate, document, and support TeXShop.
