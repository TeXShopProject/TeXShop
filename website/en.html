TeXShop

TeX and LaTeX editor for macOS

Free and open source

Download information

Originally developed by Richard Koch.

Development and maintenance are now being taken over by Yusuke Terada.

TeXShop is a native Mac application for writing TeX and LaTeX documents and previewing the typeset PDF.

Source editor

Syntax coloring, command completion, and macros for editing TeX documents.

PDF preview

View the typeset PDF alongside your source. Synchronization lets you move between corresponding locations.

Typesetting engines

Use LaTeX, pdfLaTeX, XeLaTeX, LuaLaTeX, and custom engines with a separately installed TeX distribution.

Development

TeXShop was originally developed by Richard Koch and first released in 2000. We thank him and the many contributors, translators, and users who have supported it.

The source repository begins with Richard Koch’s TeXShop 5.58 Golden Gate sources, followed by maintenance changes. Earlier development history is not included.
